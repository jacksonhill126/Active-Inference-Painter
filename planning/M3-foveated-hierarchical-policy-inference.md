# M3: Foveated Hierarchical Policy Inference

## Summary

M3 makes visual sampling an inferred action and joins gaze, marks, motor
realizations, passages, and stopping in a temporally hierarchical policy
model.

The central M3 question is:

> Does active, spatially restricted observation change the agent's beliefs and
> temporally extended mark organization in ways explained by uncertainty,
> embodiment, and hierarchical prediction?

M3 is the first milestone intended to test the project's central research
question directly. It must not hard-code gaze targets, motifs, compositional
rules, or a reward for visually interesting behavior.

## Scientific Intent

M3 tests research-charter hypotheses H1-H5:

- active foveation changes spatial organization;
- embodied prediction shapes the mark vocabulary;
- slow latent states support organization across passages;
- relational prediction can support nonsemantic composition;
- information gain alone may be insufficient or pathological.

The milestone is successful if it produces interpretable causal evidence,
including negative evidence. It is not successful merely because foveated runs
look more organized.

## Current Problems Addressed

- The agent effectively receives the whole canvas at once.
- Canvas uncertainty outside the currently relevant patch is not generated by
  an active sensor process.
- Passage plans are sampled from hand-written geometric families.
- Candidate frequency can become an undeclared policy prior.
- High-level and low-level policies do not yet form a single explicit temporal
  generative model.
- Motor forecasts are expensive and only refine a small candidate subset.
- Fixed precision and continuity parameters can dominate behavior.
- Epistemic value can favor model disagreement or novelty without producing
  useful spatial inquiry.
- Parallel experience could erase the intended single-agent developmental
  history if beliefs and parameters are pooled indiscriminately.

## Target Policy Structure

M3 should distinguish:

```text
painting-scale latent or context
        |
passage policy pi_passage
        |
mark policy pi_mark
        +---- gaze subpolicy pi_gaze
        +---- motor realization pi_motor
        |
observations and prediction errors
        |
posterior updates back through the hierarchy
```

This is a target factorization, not a requirement for one monolithic neural
network. Receding-horizon inference is acceptable if the messages, priors, and
approximations are explicit.

## Scope

### Included

- Gaze state and gaze actions.
- Foveal and peripheral observation process.
- Belief maintenance under partial observation.
- EFE for active visual sampling.
- Explicit policy hierarchy across gaze, marks, passages, and motor
  realization.
- Proposal-distribution correction or explicit policy-prior treatment.
- Conditional continuity and bodily viability priors.
- Parallel counterfactual rollout without parallel belief mixing.
- Controlled foveation, hierarchy, embodiment, and precision ablations.
- Optional frozen-encoder comparison after the from-scratch baseline.

### Deferred

- Photorealistic Isaac Sim vision unless simple foveation passes its gate.
- Human aesthetic feedback, reward models, or reference images.
- Full natural-scene understanding.
- A general artificial-life motivation system.
- Hardware camera transfer before simulation experiments identify a specific
  visual-domain limitation.
- Learned fluid handling or autonomous brush washing.

## Research Gate

M3 is the first point at which the project may cautiously interpret differences
in spatial organization as evidence about active foveation and hierarchical
inference. Interpretation still requires matched ablations, capability-gate
success, and complete run provenance.

## Feasibility

- Estimated effort: 45-72 focused workdays.
- Solo calendar estimate: 11-18 weeks.
- Target incremental training expenditure: USD 250-400.
- Keep at least USD 200 of the USD 1,000 total budget in reserve for failed
  runs, final replicas, and the optional pretrained comparison.
- The largest risks are partial-observation belief maintenance, policy-proposal
  correction, and reliable long-horizon experiments.

A smaller credible M3 stops after active gaze, partial-observation inference,
and one-mark policy selection. Hierarchical passages can remain disabled until
that version resolves ambiguity correctly. This reduces scope without
compromising the active-inference boundary.

## Failure Modes

- Calling a hard-coded saliency map active vision.
- Computing gaze information gain from hidden simulator state unavailable to
  the agent.
- Letting candidate frequency silently determine the policy prior.
- Rewarding energy efficiency so strongly that the agent stops acting.
- Treating ensemble disagreement or sensor noise as intrinsically valuable.
- Updating slow beliefs every mark until they become fast state summaries.
- Pooling online beliefs across parallel agents.
- Adding Isaac Sim before simple foveation establishes a visual-domain need.
- Selecting pretrained encoders based on attractive canvases.
- Claiming composition from one run or one seed.
